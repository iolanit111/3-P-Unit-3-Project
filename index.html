Red Velvet Cinnamon Rolls From Scratch!

  1. Recipe #1

Recipe #1

  • Time: _
  • Serves: 6
Ingredients Quantity
Milk (whole or 2%) Quantity #1
Sugar (white and brown) quanity 2
Active dry yeast quanity 3
Unsweetened coacoa powder quanity 4
Red food coloring quanity 5

Equipment:

  • Stand Mixer
  • Hand Mixer
  • Rolling Pin
  • 9x13 inch Pan
  • Oven

Step 1: Add the milk, sugar, unsalted butter, salt, yeast, and food coloring to the bowl of a stand mixer fitted with the dough hook attachment. Mix that to combine everything. Add the eggs and the sour cream and mix again. Then you will add the flour and cocoa.

Step 2: The stand mixer is now going to do all the kneading for you. Keep the mixer on medium high for about 4 minutes so it can thoroughly knead the dough. The red velvet dough should be smooth and all come together.

Step 3: Once the dough is done in the stand mixer, lightly oil a larger bowl and place the dough in the oiled bowl. Cover that will plastic wrap and let it sit at room temperature for 1-2 hours until it is doubled in size. If your house is colder, it will need a little more time, closer to 2-3 hours. It's more important that the dough has doubled in size.

Step 4: After the 2 hours is up, turn the dough onto a lightly floured surface and roll dough until it is about 18 inches by 12 inches. The dough will be very sticky, so don't be afraid to use flour when you need it.

Step 5: Making the Red Velvet Cinnamon Roll Filling. It's just brown sugar and cinnamon. You will need to melt butter and brush the butter onto the rolled out dough. Be generous here, you don't want to have any spots that aren't covered in butter on the dough. When everything is covered in butter, sprinkle the cinnamon and sugar over all of the dough.

Step 6: Once you have placed the cinnamon and sugar all over the dough, it's time to roll the dough into a large log. To do this, take the side of the dough that is closest to you and roll it up so you are rolling away from you. Try to get the dough as even in width as possible. Cut the dough into 12 even buns. The easiest way to do this is to cut the log in half. Then, place the two halves of the log right next to each other, and cut them down the middle evenly. You should now have four logs of dough (as shown below). Then do the same thing, put the four log next to each other and this time cut them into 3 pieces each, so you should end up with 12 buns.

Step 7: Place the buns into a well greased 9x13 inch baking pan and cover with plastic wrap for one hour until the are doubled in size. Bake at 375 degrees F for 35-40 minutes.

Step 8: Make the Cream Cheese Icing. Add the room temperature cream cheese and butter into a bowl and beat them until they are combined. You can do this with a stand mixer or with a hand mixer, either will work. Then add the powdered sugar, vanilla, and milk.

Step 9: Continue to beat that until it is the consistency you are looking for. If you would like it a little thinner, feel free to add more milk, and if you would like it thicker, add more sugar. Ice the buns when they are still warm so the icing melts into all the cracks of the cinnamon buns!

Helpful Tips:

  • Change it up and make these plain cinnamon buns. In order to do that, just leave out the cocoa and the red food coloring.
  • Make sure your yeast is fresh. If the yeast is expired, the dough will not rise properly.
  • Cream Cheese icing is most common for cinnamon buns like these, but you could leave out the cream cheese and just do vanilla, butter, and powdered sugar. That's delicious as well!
